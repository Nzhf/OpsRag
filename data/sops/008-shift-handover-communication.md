# SOP: Shift Handover and Communication

**Document ID:** SOP-OP-010
**Version:** 1.2
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Department:** All Production Areas
**Applies to:** SMT-1, SMT-2, SMT-3, Assembly-A, Assembly-B
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes requirements for effective shift handover communication at BizLink Penang Manufacturing. Proper handover ensures continuity of production, prevents errors, and maintains quality across shift changes.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all shift changes involving:
- Production operators and line leaders
- Quality inspectors
- Maintenance technicians
- Material handlers

## 3. Shift Schedule

| Shift | Hours | Handover Time |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Morning | 06:00 - 14:00 | 05:45 - 06:15 |
| Afternoon | 14:00 - 22:00 | 13:45 - 14:15 |
| Night | 22:00 - 06:00 | 21:45 - 22:15 |

**Handover period:** 15 minutes overlap for face-to-face communication.

## 4. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Outgoing Line Leader | Complete handover notes, brief incoming leader |
| Incoming Line Leader | Review notes, ask clarifying questions, verify status |
| Production Operator | Hand over station-specific issues to counterpart |
| Quality Inspector | Communicate quality issues, defects found |
| Material Handler | Report material status, shortages |

## 5. Handover Process

### 5.1 Preparation (15 minutes before shift end)

Outgoing shift prepares:

1. **Update production log:**
   - Units produced (good and reject)
   - Current production order status
   - Any delays or stoppages

2. **Complete Shift Handover Notes (Form SHN-001):**
   - Production status
   - Quality issues
   - Equipment status
   - Material status
   - Safety concerns
   - Pending actions

3. **Gather relevant documents:**
   - Production traveler
   - Quality log
   - Equipment status board

### 5.2 Face-to-Face Briefing (during handover period)

Standard handover topics:

**1. Production Status**
- What product are we running?
- How many units completed? Target vs. actual?
- Any schedule changes for next shift?
- Are we ahead or behind?

**2. Quality Issues**
- Any defects found this shift? Type and quantity?
- Any holds or NCRs initiated?
- First article status for new products?
- Special inspection requirements?

**3. Equipment Status**
- Any equipment problems or downtime?
- Maintenance in progress?
- Any temp fixes that need proper repair?

**4. Material Status**
- Are we running low on anything?
- Any material issues (wrong parts, quality concerns)?
- Expected deliveries?

**5. Safety and 5S**
- Any safety incidents or near-misses?
- Housekeeping status
- Any area needing attention?

**6. Pending Actions**
- What needs to be completed this shift?
- Any carry-over tasks?
- Any management requests?

### 5.3 Station-Level Handover

Individual operators hand over specific stations:

| Station | Handover Topics |
|---------|-----------------|
| Screen Printer | Stencil condition, paste age, print quality |
| Pick and Place | Feeder setup, nozzle condition, reject rate |
| Reflow | Profile being used, any oven issues |
| AOI | False call rate, program loaded |
| Inspection | Defects found, inspection criteria |
| Rework | Units in queue, rework difficulty |
| Test | Pass/fail rate, test issues |

### 5.4 Documentation

- Incoming Line Leader signs handover notes to acknowledge receipt
- Handover notes retained at line for 7 days
- Significant issues copied to Shift Supervisor report

## 6. Escalation During Handover

Issues requiring immediate escalation to Shift Supervisor:

- Equipment down with no estimated fix time
- Material shortage affecting production within 4 hours
- Quality issue requiring line stoppage
- Safety hazard identified
- Major schedule change (product switch, rush order)

## 7. Absent Operator Protocol

If an operator does not report for shift:

1. Line Leader attempts contact (phone per emergency contact list)
2. If no response in 15 minutes, notify HR
3. Assign temporary coverage from available float operators
4. Document absence in attendance log
5. Handover includes: "X operator absent, Y covering station 3"

## 8. Communication Tools

### 8.1 Shift Handover Notes (SHN-001)

Standard form capturing:
- Date and shift
- Line and product
- Production summary
- Issues log
- Pending actions
- Signatures (outgoing and incoming)

### 8.2 Visual Management

Each line has a communication board showing:
- Current production status (hourly count)
- Quality metrics (FPY, defects)
- Equipment status (green/yellow/red indicators)
- Safety alerts
- Upcoming events (audits, visitors, training)

### 8.3 Digital Communication

- Teams channel for each production line
- Post urgent issues with @mention for supervisors
- Photos of defects or equipment issues encouraged
- Shift summary posted by end of each shift

## 9. Training

All production personnel receive handover training including:
- This SOP
- How to complete handover notes
- Face-to-face communication best practices
- Escalation procedures

**Training completion:** Documented in training records

## 10. Audit and Compliance

- Shift Supervisors verify handover notes completed daily
- Weekly spot-check of handover quality by Production Manager
- Monthly review of communication-related incidents
- Corrective action for repeated handover failures

## 11. Continuous Improvement

Handover effectiveness reviewed in:
- Weekly production meetings
- Shift supervisor debriefs
- Employee feedback

Common improvement actions:
- Updated handover checklist
- Enhanced visual management
- Simplified documentation
